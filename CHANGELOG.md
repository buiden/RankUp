# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- First release.
- Checklist of your class's active abilities (/rankup or /ru, or the minimap addon menu).
- Ticked spells are upgraded to their highest rank on every action bar when you train a new rank, when you tick them, and at login.
- Upgrades wait until combat ends and never drop what you're holding on the cursor.
- Choices saved per character.
